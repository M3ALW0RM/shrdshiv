Running shards, for beginners

Notes from Heron. Printed because you'll lose a message but you won't lose paper.

What you've got

  • Your deck, the DS-4. A shard goes in the SHARD slot, a cracker cartridge goes in the AUX port.
  • Your shards, the little data sticks in your stash. Most of them are locked. Locked shards are what you get paid to open.
  • Your cartridges. You start with SHRDSHIV. Its README is on your bookcase.

Click a shard in your stash to put it in the deck. Click a cartridge to plug it in or pull it out. You can type on the deck's keys or use your keyboard: Enter is #, Esc or Backspace is *.

Reading a shard

With no cartridge plugged in, the deck runs Kestrel's own reader, ShardView. If a shard isn't locked, ShardView reads it for you.

Shard 0042 Text document Integrity: verified 1-read 2-eject

Every screen ends with its keys like that: the key, a dash, what it does. 1 reads, 2 ejects.

Cracking a shard

Plug in SHRDSHIV, then insert a locked shard. You get the map:

SHRDSHIV :: 0588 enc SEGLOCK (blocks) juice 12 ██████████ [1] lock d? ????? [2] lock d? ????? [3] lock d? ????? 1-crack 2-snif 3-warez 4-read *-eject
  1. Each line is a sector, one lock on the shard. d is how hard the lock is, from d1 to d6.
  2. Press 1 to crack, then the sector's number.
  3. Type a guess at the code and press #. The deck tells you how close you were.
  4. Keep guessing until it opens. Then press 4 to read what you freed.

On d3 and harder, the lock only tells you how many are right, not which ones. That's when better crackers earn their price.

Juice

Every guess costs 1 juice. Every shard has a fixed amount, shared by all its sectors. When the juice runs out, every sector still locked burns to static and is gone. That's called frying a shard.

2 sniffs a lock: you spend juice to learn things about it, like how long the code is or how many symbols it uses. 3 opens your warez, one-shot programs you buy from Rook. Sniffing is worth it on hard locks. On a d1, just guess.

Getting paid

  • Rook pays when a shard is done: nothing left locked. Harder sectors pay more.
  • Open every sector without frying any and she adds a clean-job bonus.
  • If you fry a shard, she only pays for sectors you opened that can still be read on their own. Some ciphers, like CHAFF and COMB, scatter the text across every sector. Half of one of those is worth nothing.
  • If a shard is going bad, hand it back before you fry it. The job bar under your deck shows what she'll pay. The more you've opened and the more juice is left, the more she pays, because her next runner can still finish it.

Hand it back early, not with 1 juice left. A shard with nothing left in it is worth nothing to anyone.

Heat

The DS-4 runs hot when it works hard. Watch the TEMP readout under the screen. At 70 °C the overheat light comes on. At 85 °C it shuts itself off until it cools. Cheap software runs cool. Fancy software doesn't.

Rook's counter

Once you've done a job for her, her counter opens: top right, the button with the cart. She sells crackers, gear for your deck and warez, and she gets new stock as you prove yourself. Everything you buy comes with its manual. Read them.

She also hands out side jobs if you ask. Small stuff, good practice, honest money.

Heron's rules

  1. Read the map before you touch a lock.
  2. Crack the easy sectors first. They pay the same whether you're scared or not.
  3. Never spend your last juice on a guess you can't afford to lose.
  4. Don't trust anyone who calls you choom.
  5. Pay Rook what you owe her. Always.

Good luck, kid. Don't call me unless it's bad. Heron.
