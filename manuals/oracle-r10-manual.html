ORACLE//ZERO r10 XJ

The ORACLE engine rebuilt for Tianshu's Xuanji OS. Same analysis, full graphical interface, and a thermal profile the ARCHON-7 can live with.

BUILD r10.3-XJTARGET TIANSHU ARCHON-7 · XJ3SUPERSEDES r9 (DS-4)

1What changed from r9

  • Native XJ3 build. r10 runs directly on Xuanji OS. It does not go through the DS-4 emulator, so it no longer overheats the host.
  • Graphical interface. The full ARCHON-7 screen: a map of the shard, a lock view with candidate counts, and a log window.
  • Same engine. Estimates, the solver and zero-trace cracking behave exactly as in r9.
  • Not for the DS-4. A DS-4 rejects r10. Keep r9 for consumer hardware.

2On insert

ORACLE identifies the shard's cipher by its real designation, with its origin and how confident the identification is, then shows every sector's difficulty and contents automatically. You never pay for a map or content scan.

3Cracking a lock

  • Feedback is always positional, even on locks that only report totals. Marks: ■ right symbol in the right place, □ right symbol in the wrong place, . not in the code.
  • Symbols that have been ruled out are struck out for you.
  • ORACLE counts the codes still possible after every guess and estimates how many guesses each sector will take.

4The solver

Press # three times with nothing typed to ask for a suggestion. ORACLE proposes a full next guess for 2 juice, up to 3 times per shard, once per guess.

WITH A CO-PROCESSOR the solver becomes oracle-class: suggestions are free and unlimited, but they are blocked on level 6 locks and may trip countermeasures on level 5.

5Zero trace

Sectors opened by ORACLE carry no residue. The owner's reader shows no integrity error for them.
