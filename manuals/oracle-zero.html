1.0Scope

they built a crowbar and wrote "not a crowbar" on it.

ORACLE//ZERO is the Kestrel DataSystems counter-intrusion suite. It is issued to authorised analysts for the recovery of encrypted media from compromised sources, and for the assessment of third-party ciphers encountered in the field. Possession outside Kestrel premises constitutes a breach of contract, a violation of the Corporate Assets Act, and grounds for immediate termination of the holder.

Analysts are reminded that the suite is a recovery tool. It is not to be used to read media belonging to Kestrel or its partners without a ticket.

Press # and the remaining code is entered and submitted automatically.

6.0Decision model

it's good. it's too good. three uses is plenty. learn to think without it or you'll be lost the day it's gone.

The suite's decision model recommends the submission most likely to narrow the field. To request a recommendation, press # three times with no input.

  • Cost: 2 budget per recommendation.
  • Limit: 3 per media, and one per submission.

With an Analysis Co-processor installed, the model operates at oracle-class: no cost and no limit. Oracle-class is withheld on protection level 6. On protection level 5, use carries a 1 in 4 probability of detection by the media's ICE, in which case the lock is rotated and all progress on that block is voided.

7.0Zero trace

this is the whole reason to steal it.

Blocks recovered by ORACLE//ZERO leave no residue. Corporate readers continue to report the media as sealed, and fully recovered media reports Integrity: verified. The owner will never know.

Auxiliary programs (bypass, bruteforce, scrapper) do leave forensic residue. The suite will warn the analyst before executing them.
