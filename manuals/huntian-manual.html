HUNTIAN 1.0 Handbook

A cracker that reads the whole shard as a sky. Written by Lin Yuhan, formerly of Tianshu Systems. For the ARCHON-7.

要旨Why HUNTIAN is different

Every other cracker picks the locks, one sector at a time. HUNTIAN ignores the locks entirely and attacks the cipher underneath. It never touches a lock, so it never leaves a trace.

"A lock is a door. A cipher is a language. Learn the language and every door in the house opens." Lin Yuhan

It shines on long text shards with many sectors, where the same letters repeat again and again. On a short shard there is less to work with.

天球The sphere

HUNTIAN shows the shard as a slowly turning sphere of glyphs. Each sector has its own letter substitution: within a sector, one glyph always stands for the same letter. Glyphs are the 28 lunar mansions:

角亢氐房心尾箕斗牛女虚危室壁奎娄胃昴毕觜参井鬼柳星张翼轸

Numbers are written with the ten Heavenly Stems. Numbers share one table across the whole shard, so a digit solved once is solved everywhere.

甲0乙1丙2丁3戊4己5庚6辛7壬8癸9

Switch the interface between 中文 and English with the language key on the side of the map.

三法Three ways to learn a letter

  1. Frequency analysis, free. HUNTIAN marks the three most common glyphs with the letter they most likely stand for. Testing a suggestion is free if it's right and costs 1 attempt if it's wrong.
  2. Locks. Pick a glyph and open a lock on it. Opening the lock costs 1 attempt. Inside, you play a code game, like on any cracker, and the guesses inside it are free. The more often a glyph appears, the harder its lock. HUNTIAN counts the codes still possible and fills in the code once only one is left: press Submit to open it.
  3. Cribs. Guess a word of 4 or more characters that you think is in the shard. Type it phone-style on keys 2 to 9, or on your keyboard. SUGGEST offers dictionary words that fit a half-solved word. A right crib is free and fills in every letter it covers. A wrong one costs 1 attempt.

A sector opens when every glyph in it is resolved. Start with the free hints, use cribs once words take shape, and save locks for the stubborn glyphs.

注意Before you start

  • HUNTIAN runs only on the ARCHON-7.
  • Attempts come out of the shard's juice like any guess. When the juice is gone, the unread sectors are lost.
  • On audio shards there is no text to read, so a lock-picking cracker may serve you better.
