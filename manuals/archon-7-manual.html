Tianshu ARCHON-7 User Guide

Runner-grade cyberdeck. Xuanji OS 玄机. Designed in Shenzhen for people who need to see everything at once.

EXPORT EDITION · XJ3

概述Overview

The ARCHON-7 is a full workstation in a deck. It runs Xuanji OS on a multi-window colour screen, has seven programmable side keys and five hardware bays, and runs cooler and longer than consumer decks.

With no software in the AUX port, it opens shards with DataView 数据视图, Tianshu's graphical bilingual reader. Shards, keys and status work as they do on any licensed deck.

屏幕The screen

MAINThe running program: DataView, or the cartridge in the AUX port.
SYSTemperature, hardware bays and the module in the AUX port.
MEDIAWhat's playing, for voice recordings.
LOGA running log of what the deck does.
  • Click a side window's title bar to collapse it onto the sidebar. Click its tab on the sidebar to bring it back.
  • Native software can use the whole screen and open its own windows.

侧键Side keys

Seven keys run down the side of the screen. Each has a small display that shows what the key does right now, and their jobs change with what's on screen: confirm, back, page, language, and whatever the running software needs.

The keypad works as on any deck: 0 to 9 and A to F, # to confirm and * to go back.

兼容性Compatibility

SoftwareOn the ARCHON-7
Native XJ3 softwareRuns directly with full colour and windows.
DS-4 cartridgesRun through the built-in DS-4 emulator, built on shared libraries. They look and behave exactly as on a DS-4.
Some heavy DS-4 softwareMay overload the emulator's host. The governor shuts the deck down, cools it and ejects the cartridge.

Note. Native ARCHON-7 software does not run on other decks.

扩展槽Hardware bays

Five bays, against the two on a typical consumer deck. Any standard hardware module fits and works with any software. Modules can be moved between decks.

散热Heat

  • The SYS window shows the deck's temperature. It turns red at 85 °C.
  • At 100 °C the thermal governor shuts the deck down, runs its cooling screen and ejects the cartridge.
  • The deck resumes at 50 °C. Re-insert the cartridge to continue.
