words this thing uses

on screenwhat it means
seca sector. a shard is a bunch of these, each one its own lock
juicehow many tries the shard has left. not yours, the shard's. waste it and the next person's screwed
openyou cracked it. it stays cracked
lockstill locked
BURNstatic. gone. the shard ran dry while it was still locked

the map

this is home. every time you plug in a shard, this is what you see:

SHRDSHIV :: 0417
enc SEGLOCK (blocks)
juice 16 ██████████
[1] lock   d? ?????
[2] open   PLACE
[3] BURN   ~~~~~~
1crak 2snif 3warez
4play *eject
  • 1 crak: pick a sec and start guessing.
  • 2 snif: spend juice to learn about a sec before you hit it. sometimes smart, sometimes a waste.
  • 3 warez: your one-shot toys from Mama Rook or wherever.
  • 4 play / read: listen to or read whatever you've opened so far. locked bits come out as static. still fun.

the d? is how hard the lock is. SHRDSHIV doesn't know unless you sniff it or your deck has the hardware for it.

the marks

every guess you type gets marked:

markmeaning
●right symbol, right spot
○right symbol, wrong spot
·not in there at all

on easy locks the marks line up under your guess so you know which spot was right. on hard locks you just get a pile, like ●○○, and you have to work out which is which. that's the game. get a notebook. i'm serious, get a notebook.

what it doesn't do

no analyzer. no list of what you've ruled out. no tags. no probe. no guessing for you. i get asked about this a lot. the answer is no. a good hacker on SHRDSHIV beats a lazy one on anything they could buy.
