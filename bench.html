How it plays

      

        
Corporate reader. With no cracker plugged in, the deck runs KESTREL ShardView. A shard is either readable, encrypted, or flagged with an integrity error if someone has cracked part of it. Every shard has a 6-digit key (listed on the shard card for testing).

        
Crackers. Four cracker cartridges, each with its own personality and built-in features. Plug one in from the cartridge tray; plugging another swaps it. The cracker sees the shard's sectors and its juice: the attempts left on that shard, shared by everyone who ever works on it.

        
Cracking a sector. Guess the hidden code. ● means right symbol, right place. ○ means right symbol, wrong place. At easy levels feedback lines up with your guess; at hard ones you only get the counts. Every guess costs 1 juice.

        
Sniffing. Spend juice for information: a sector's code length, what it holds, which is hardest, how many symbols it uses, or every sector's difficulty.

        
Playback. Audio shards don't autoplay. Press # for the next sector, B for the previous one, 0 to replay, or a sector number to jump straight to it. Locked and burned sectors play as static.

        
Text shards. Read with # and B to turn pages. In the cracker, press a sector number to show only that sector's characters, and 0 to show everything. Text shards use three encryption schemes: whole blocks, every nth character, or words scattered at random. The cracker names the scheme on its map.

        
Out of juice. Every sector still locked burns to static for good. Cracked sectors stay open on the shard.

        
Warez. Probe reveals one symbol. Bypass opens an easy sector for 2 juice. Brute force opens any sector, but the rest re-encrypt one level harder. Scrapper burns a sector for extra juice. Reset refills the shard's juice. 

        
Probe. Press # three times in a row with nothing typed to ask the cracker for a suggestion. Level 1 (LA VEUVE) suggests symbols to test, costs 1 juice, 2 uses per shard. Level 2 (RAZORBACK) fills half the positions for 1 juice. It charges up as your guesses narrow things down, shown as a meter, and unlocks once 500 or fewer codes remain. Level 3 (ORACLE//ZERO) gives a full guess for 2 juice, 3 uses per shard, once per guess. A co-processor makes suggestions smarter and turns Oracle's probe oracle-class: free and unlimited, but blocked on level 6 locks and liable to trip ICE on level 5. Probe uses are counted per shard, like juice. The Insight warez gives any cracker one sharp full suggestion.

        
Spread shot. With RAZORBACK, choosing crack lets you load up to 3 locked sectors of the same difficulty. Each guess is fired at all of them and costs 1 ammo per target still standing, and the screen shows each target's feedback in its own column. Cleared targets drop out with a shotgun rack.

        
Hardware vs software. The deck's hardware (rating, auto-scan, full map, content tags, cheap scans) stays with the deck whichever cracker is plugged in. The analyzer and elimination log only come with software. Full map, content tags and cheap scans can come from either; cheap scans stack. Software can't be upgraded, only replaced by a better cracker.
